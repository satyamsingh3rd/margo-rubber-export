faqs:
  - q: Which compound do you recommend for outdoor duct sealing?
    a: >-
      EPDM is the usual choice for weather-exposed sealing thanks to its UV
      and ozone resistance. Silicone is available where higher temperature
      tolerance is required.
  - q: Can you supply flame-retardant grommets?
    a: >-
      Flame-retardant and low-smoke compounds are available on request for
      enclosure and panel applications. Send your standard reference with
      the enquiry.
  - q: How are anti-vibration mounts specified?
    a: >-
      By static load rating and deflection at that load, plus mounting
      geometry and durometer. Send the machine mass and mounting pattern
      with the drawing.
  - q: What is the minimum order quantity?
    a: >-
      500 pieces per part number, per material. Catalogue sizes ship in 14
      to 21 days ex-works Nashik from drawing approval.
  - q: Do you supply foam as well as solid profiles?
    a: >-
      Yes. Both foam and solid gasket profiles are available, selected by
      the compression set and sealing force your application requires.
