scope:
  eyebrow: CERTIFICATION SCOPE
  heading: What ISO 9001:2015 Covers, and What It Does Not
  meansHeading: What this certification means
  means:
    - Our Quality Management System (QMS) has been independently audited and meets ISO 9001:2015 requirements.
    - Documented processes govern order intake, production scheduling, incoming material inspection, in-process monitoring, final inspection, and dispatch.
    - We maintain corrective action records, management review cycles, and continual-improvement objectives.
    - The certificate is issued by an accredited third-party Certification Body and subject to periodic surveillance audits.

  notHeading: What this certification does not mean
  notLead: Margo Rubber Products is not IATF 16949 certified.
  notLeadBody: >-
    IATF 16949 is an automotive-sector-specific standard that builds on ISO 9001
    with additional requirements for automotive supply chains. We do not hold
    this certification. If your sourcing policy requires IATF 16949, we are not
    the right supplier for that requirement.
  notItems:
    - ISO 9001:2015 does not specify product performance, dimensional tolerances, or material properties, those are governed by our product specifications and your drawings.
    - Certification does not imply approval for any specific regulated end-use application (medical, aerospace, defence) unless separately agreed in writing.
    - We do not hold any other management system certifications at this time (e.g. ISO 14001, ISO 45001).
  footnote: >-
    If you are conducting a supplier qualification audit, we recommend
    downloading the certificate below and reviewing it against your
    approved-supplier checklist. For audit scheduling or additional QMS
    documentation requests, use the contact form at the bottom of this page.
