faqs:
  - q: What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
    a: >-
      For standard catalogue products like O-rings and oil seals, our MOQ is
      typically 100 pieces. For custom molded parts requiring tooling, MOQ
      varies by product complexity, generally 500 to 1,000 pieces. We do
      accommodate sample orders of 10 to 50 pieces for engineering evaluation.
  - q: Can you manufacture to customer drawings and specifications?
    a: >-
      Yes. Send your part drawing or a physical sample for evaluation and we
      respond with a quotation and feasibility report. Tooling is designed and
      owned in-house, so changes are made on-site rather than through a
      third-party toolmaker.
  - q: What rubber materials are available, and how do I choose the right one?
    a: >-
      We compound six core elastomers in-house: NBR, EPDM, silicone, neoprene,
      FKM and natural rubber. Selection is driven by service temperature, the
      media in contact, and required hardness. Our engineering team will
      recommend a compound against your application.
  - q: Do you supply standard dimensional series like AS568 and metric O-rings?
    a: >-
      Yes. We manufacture to AS568, ISO 3601, JIS B2401 and DIN 3771, covering
      the four major global catalogues. Non-catalogue and cut-to-print sizes are
      also available from approved drawings.
  - q: What is your typical lead time for orders?
    a: >-
      Catalogue sizes ship in 14 to 21 days ex-works Nashik from drawing
      approval. Custom and cut-to-print parts take 21 to 35 days, subject to
      tooling requirement.
  - q: Do you provide material certifications and test reports?
    a: >-
      Yes. Every production batch is validated in our in-house laboratory and
      supplied with material and dimensional test documentation, with full
      traceability from raw compound to dispatch.
  - q: Can you supply globally, and what are your export capabilities?
    a: >-
      We export from Nashik, India to buyers across our active markets, with
      Incoterms FOB Mumbai / Nhava Sheva as standard and CIF or DDP on request.
  - q: What quality standards does Margo Rubber operate to?
    a: >-
      Margo Rubber Products operates a documented Quality Management System
      certified to ISO 9001:2015, covering order intake, production scheduling,
      incoming material inspection, in-process monitoring, final inspection and
      dispatch.
