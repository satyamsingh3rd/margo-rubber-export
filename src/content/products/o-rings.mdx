  answer: >-

    - code: ISO 3601
      name: International Metric Standard
      body: >-
        Global metric catalogue in three parts covering dimensions (Part 1),
        tolerances (Part 2), and quality acceptance (Part 3).
    - code: JIS B2401
      name: Japanese Industrial Standard
      body: >-
        Japanese metric series, P, G, V, S size groups, commonly required by
        Japanese OEM supply chains.
    - code: DIN 3771
      name: German Industrial Standard
      body: >-
        DIN metric catalogue, harmonised into ISO 3601 but still actively called
        out in German mechanical engineering drawings.
