faqs:
  - q: What is the difference between closed-cell and open-cell foam rubber?
    a: >-
      Closed-cell foam has sealed membranes, so it absorbs under 1% water by
      volume and recovers fully from compression — the choice for weatherseals
      and insulation. Open-cell foam has interconnected pores, giving 0.6–0.9
      NRC acoustic damping but higher compression set. Cell structure is the
      first decision, before compound or density.
  - q: How is foam density specified, and why does it matter?
    a: >-
      Density is quoted in kg/m³ and ranges from 12 to 200. It determines
      whether a foam seals, insulates or absorbs: 12–30 kg/m³ is acoustic and
      packaging foam, 30–60 is HVAC duct liner, 60–100 is weatherseal and
      gasketing, and 100–200 is structural. Specify it alongside compound
      rather than after.
